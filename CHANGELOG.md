# Changelog

From 1.0.0 on, every release is listed on this repository's Releases page, with notes taken from
its pull request. The `CHANGELOG.md` inside each release zip has the full history.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-17

First release.

- A sky orb on the minimap: the time of day, the moon's real phase, and the weather drawn over it
  (cloud, mist, fog, rain, storms, snow, haze, sandstorms, ashfall, miasma), day and night.
- A compact readout beside the minimap: temperature, wind, humidity and air pressure, with a warm /
  cold badge based on how it feels.
- A climate for every zone. Weather comes from the zone and the realm clock, so everyone sees the
  same sky, and it changes slowly over hours.
- Inns and city buildings are heated; fires and forges warm you, and are remembered.
- A tooltip with the feels-like temperature, wind, barometer trend, moon phase and a 3-hour outlook.
- Commands: `/imw` (or `/weather`); `/imw help` lists them all.
